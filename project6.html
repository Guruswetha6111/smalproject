A content framework is a structured plan that guides the creation, management, and implementation of content. It acts as a blueprint, ensuring consistent, high-quality, and purposeful content by outlining strategies, procedures, and specifications for content creators. This framework helps align content with business objectives, improve audience engagement, and drive desired actions. Key aspects of a content framework: Strategy: Defines the "why," "how," and "who" of content creation, including target audience, goals, and key performance indicators (KPIs). Structure: Provides a system for organizing content, including content types, formats, and distribution channels. Process: Outlines the workflow for content creation, from planning and production to publishing and promotion. Quality Assurance: Establishes standards and guidelines to ensure content meets specific quality criteria and achieves desired outcomes.
